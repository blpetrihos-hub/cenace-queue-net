Place in line for electricity

CENACE interconnection and connection queues, Mexico. Diagnostic only.

Dashboard Methods Bibliography

What this measures

U.S. and PRC-tied projects in Mexico compete for the same grid. The thing you can count is place in CENACE’s published interconnection and connection queues: megawatts, municipality, request date, study status. It is not industrial-park announcements, not IMMEX clocks, and not water titles.

A map alone cannot show waiting time or study status. A table alone cannot show that Nuevo León and the Bajío are the same physical bottleneck. The dashboard holds both. The clock is 2026–2036. Nothing here is an influence score, and nothing here is a recommendation.

What CENACE publishes

CENACE posts, on a statutory cadence, workbooks of the interconnection cola (generators, registro prefix SICE) and the connection cola (load, prefix SCCC). The live listing is the year dropdown on Solicitud de Conexión e Interconexión. Directory indexes return 403; files are retrieved from the ASP.NET listing. Filenames use accented Interconexión / Conexión.

Columns actually present in the 2021–2026 workbooks: número de registro, fecha de registro, estatus, fecha de estado, fecha estimada de operación normal, capacidad (MW), tecnología, municipio, entidad federativa, GCR (regional control gerencia). Company name is not in that list. SIASIC may collect razón social; the public cola does not. There is also no substation “punto de interconexión” field. Geography on the map is the municipality of record, not a bus name.

Study-status values observed: Recepción y revisión, Aceptada, Observada, Atendida, Cancelada, and (in older files) Revisión. “In queue” on this site means the first three plus Revisión. Atendida is treated as already processed, not waiting. Cancelada is cancelled. Historical attended MW is kept in the tidy file and excluded from the map of who is still in line.

mw_days

For each row, days_in_queue is snapshot date minus fecha de solicitud (floored at zero). Snapshot date is parsed from the title row (“Información actualizada al: 28 de febrero de 2026”), not from file mtime. mw_days = MW × days_in_queue. Rows with MW ≥ 5,000 are flagged capacity_outlier and dropped from totals (two cells in the latest generation file are 308,244 MW and 531,000 MW — unit or digit errors, not plants).

Geography

Municipal seats are a checked-in gazetteer (data/gazetteer/nodes.csv), filled from known cabecera coordinates for industrial municipios. Remaining rows fall back to the state centroid and are tagged state_centroid. Rows whose entidad federativa cannot be parsed (including a handful of “Texas” and numeric junk) stay on the unlocated-MW statistic and are not dropped.

Ownership

Matching is a small human codebook, not a scrape of Orbis. A cola row is classified U.S. / PRC / Mexico / other only when a reputable source ties that municipality (and, where possible, MW and technology) to a named firm, then UBO is coded. Confidence is confirmed or probable. Everyone else is unmatched and remains visible. The dashboard can hide unmatched MW so you can see how little of the cola is actually named; it cannot invent names.

v1 codebook is four rows: two probable Tesla loads in Santa Catarina (one still in queue, one already Atendida), a probable LGMG load in Marín, and a confirmed CFE 2.1 GW plant at Tuxpan. Hofusan in Salinas Victoria is in the bibliography and not in the codebook: El Economista names the park and the PRC partners, not megawatts or a registro.

Every classified map point and codebook row has a source_id that exists in sources/bibliography.yml. The bibliography tab is generated from that file.

What would falsify the claim

  • The queue does not bind: interconnection/connection studies are not the constraint on who energizes, so place-in-line is the wrong clock.
  • Names cannot be joined: if the public cola stays anonymous and no reputable source can tie node + MW to a firm, the U.S. vs PRC comparison is a handful of probable rows against a sea of unmatched MW — which is what the unmatched filter is for.
  • Off-list CFE or private deals energize without appearing in these workbooks; the dashboard would then undercount the real line.
  • Capacity outliers are not errors but a different unit; we would then be excluding real GW.
