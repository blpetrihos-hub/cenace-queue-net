Annotated bibliography

Official sources, named academic publishers for context, and established outlets for named factory matches. Built from sources/bibliography.yml so this page cannot drift.

Excluded: World Population Review, anonymous blogs, SOUTHCOM advocacy as finding, AMP-style clips.

Official

official · cenace_colas

Centro Nacional de Control de Energía (CENACE). “Solicitud de Conexión e Interconexión.” Public interconnection and connection queues (CSV/XLS posted by year). Mexico City: CENACE.

https://www.cenace.gob.mx/Paginas/SIM/SolicitudConexion.aspx

Primary data. CENACE is required to post, at least every two months, capacity, technology, location, estimated COD, request date, and study status. Company name is not in that mandated list. This project scrapes the year tabs 2021–2026 for both interconnection (generators) and connection (load). Snapshot date is the title-row update date, not file mtime.

Supports: queue

official · cne_permisos_csv

Comisión Nacional de Energía (CNE). “Permisos de generación vigentes por modalidad.” CSV. Mexico City: CNE.

https://www.cne.gob.mx/da/PermisosdeGeneracionVigentesporModalidad.csv

Named-owner source for generators. Lists permit-holder, authorized MW, technology, state, address, lead company, and country of origin. Joined onto in-queue SICE rows only when state + MW rounded to one decimal + technology family is unique on both sides; a tighter key adds municipality when both sides have it. Mixed labels such as Canada and China are Other, not PRC. Country of origin can hide PRC capital behind a Mexican company; that hole stays on Mexico or unnamed bars. Load (SCCC) is not in this file.

Supports: queue

official · ley_sector_electrico_2025

México. Ley del Sector Eléctrico. Diario Oficial de la Federación, 18 March 2025.

https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/ref/lse.htm

In force. Replaces the Electric Industry Law as the statute for the national electric system, CENACE’s control function, and interconnection/connection procedures. Cited for what the queue is, not as a count of megawatts.

Supports: methods

official · ley_industria_electrica

México. Ley de la Industria Eléctrica (abrogated). Diario Oficial de la Federación, 11 August 2014; abrogated 18 March 2025.

https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/abro/lielec.htm

The statute that created the public queue obligation for the snapshots scraped here through 2025. Kept so a reader can see the legal clock; we do not treat the abrogated text as current procedure.

Supports: methods

official · sener_convocatoria_2025_10

Secretaría de Energía. “Convocatoria para la atención prioritaria de solicitudes de permisos de generación eléctrica e interconexión al Sistema Eléctrico Nacional, alineados a la planeación vinculante.” Diario Oficial de la Federación, 17 October 2025 (evening edition).

https://www.dof.gob.mx/

Official clock on who gets preferred attention at CNE/CENACE after October 2025. Context for why the queue and this call for projects are not the same list. We do not scrape call winners as if they were the queue.

Supports: methods

Secretaría de Energía. “Acuerdo por el que se emite la modificación a la Convocatoria para la atención prioritaria de solicitudes de permisos de generación eléctrica e interconexión al Sistema Eléctrico Nacional, alineados a la planeación vinculante.” Diario Oficial de la Federación, 24 October 2025.

https://sidof.segob.gob.mx/notas/docFuente/5774850

official · sener_convocatoria_2026_05

Secretaría de Energía. “Convocatoria para la atención de proyectos estratégicos de generación y almacenamiento de energía eléctrica, alineados a la planeación vinculante.” Diario Oficial de la Federación, 15 May 2026.

https://www.dof.gob.mx/

Second official window (May 2026) for strategic generation and storage, including interconnection/connection studies. Cited as the state process running in parallel with the public queue. Modified 26 May 2026 and again 10 July 2026; those orders change calendars, not our MW counts.
